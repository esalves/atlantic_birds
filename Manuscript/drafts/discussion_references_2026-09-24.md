## Notes

- **Years.** Crossref gives the online year for two entries. The bib uses the print year: Scheffers 2014 (online 2013) and Yom-Tov & Geffen 2011 (online 2010).
- **Search error corrected.** The search agent summarised Desrochers 2010 the wrong way round. I corrected it against the abstract: wings became *more* pointed in boreal species under clear-cutting, and less pointed in temperate forest species.
- **Found but not used:**
  - de la Hera et al. 2009 (*Biol J Linn Soc* 97:98–105): Crossref has no abstract, so its content could not be checked.
  - Bélisle & Desrochers 2002 and Robertson & Radford 2009 (gap crossing): redundant with Claramunt 2022.
- **Searched but not verified, and not used:**
  - "Riemer et al." meta-analysis of Bergmann's rule.
  - "Moore et al. 2008" on gap crossing.
  - Salewski & Watt's review of Bergmann's rule.
  - Lens et al. on fragmentation and wing shape.
- **Possible additions if a co-author wants them:**
  - A Bergmann/body-size review specific to birds (e.g. Salewski & Watt), once verified.
  - An Atlantic Forest reference on microclimate and edge effects. The ones cited are Amazonian or global.
